ClassicTanks

ClassicTanks is a free top-down arcade tank game for Android phones. Take command of an armored vehicle, fight through 40 stages, beat massive bosses and upgrade your tanks.

Android  ·  In testing, coming to Google Play

What ClassicTanks does

The purpose of the ClassicTanks app is entertainment: it is a single-player mobile game. You drive a tank with on-screen controls, destroy enemy vehicles and earn in-game credits to upgrade your vehicles. Your progress is stored on your device.

ClassicTanks can use Google Play Games Services for sign-in with your Play Games profile, achievements and leaderboards. Your player profile is used only for these game features. The app shows ads through Google AdMob. It does not generate, collect or share images of people. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

The battlefield

What's inside

40 stages

Rivers, bridges, brick walls, concrete, sandbags and bushes to hide in.

8 boss battles

Commander-General, Twin Fortress, artillery and an armored train, plus tougher versions.

5 vehicles

From a light tank to a twin-cannon heavy. Amphibious vehicles swim across rivers.

Supply crates

Repairs, rapid fire, armor shields, splash shells and armor-piercing rounds.

Upgrades

Earn credits and improve armor, speed, rate of fire and turret traverse.

Touch controls

Twin sticks with aim assist, made for short sessions on your phone.
